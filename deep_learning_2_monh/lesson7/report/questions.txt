1. Data augmentation va transforms ni nima farqi va oxshashlik tomoni bor?
=> Data Augmentation datani xilma xil qilish. Ko'paytirish. mavjud bolgan datadan. 
Faqat train datani ko'paytiriladi.
=> Transform esa Data Augmenttion uchun vosita.
Train va test datada ham qollanilad

2. Ularni qanday foydali va zararli tomonlari bor
=> Data Augmentation
- Model real o'zgarishlarga chidamli boladi (bitta datani Har tomondan korinishini oladi), Data kam bo'lganda kopaytira olamiz, Overfittingni kamaytiradi
- Label manosi buzilib qolishi mumkin (6 va 9 ni rotate qilish), Yangi haqiqiy malumotlar qoshmaydi, Train sekinlashadi, Har rasm qayta ishlanishi hisobiga
=> Transforms
- Hamma rasm bir xil shaklda 200 x 200 bo'ladi, Normalization trainni tezlashtiradi
- Resize detalni yo'qotadi, proporsiyani buzishi mumkin, Crop muhim qismni kesib tashlashi mumkin

3. Qaysi biri nima uchun foydalaniladi
- Rasmni model kiritishiga moslash (o'lcham, tensor, normalize) - transforms (Resize, ToImage, Normalize)
- Kam data bilan overfitting ni kamaytirish - Augmentation (RandomHorizontalFlip, RandomRotation)
- Yorug'lik va rang o'zgarish - Color augmentation (ColorJitter)
- Kamera burchagi va masofasi o'zgarishiga chidamlilik - Geometric augmentation (RandomAffine, RandomResizedCrop)

Transform - ham trainda, ham test dataset uchun ishlatiladi
Augmentation - faqat trainda, modelga oqitish jarayonida 

4. Nima uchun Data Augmentation uchun OpenCV yoki Pillow dan foydalanmasdan Torchvisiondan foydalandik
Pillow va OpenCV rasm bilan ishlash vositalari (flip, rotate vahokazo). Torchvision esa PyTorch uchun tayyor augmentation tizimi (random, ketma-ketlik, dataset bilan ulanish bor).
- RandomHorizontalFlip(p=0.5), RandomRotation(15). Pillow va OpenCV da "50% holatda flip qilish" ni o'zimiz random bilan yozishimiz kerak.
- Compose - Bir nechta amalni bitta zanjirga ulaydi.
- PyTorch modeli tensor kutadi. ToImage, ToDtype, Normalize shu yerning o'zida.

=> Pillow va OpenCV = alohida asboblar (bolg'a, arra). Torchvision = shu asboblar yig'ilgan, PyTorch ga moslangan tayyor ustaxona.
